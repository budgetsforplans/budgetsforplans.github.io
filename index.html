💬 Ask Your Own Budget

"What did I spend most on last month?" Ask puts the question in plain language and answers from the figures already in your plans — generated on your device by Apple Intelligence.

📱

Runs on your device

Answers are generated on your iPhone or iPad. Your entries, plans, and questions are never sent to a server, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

🔢

The app does the maths

Every figure is measured by Budgets For Plans itself, the same way the rest of the app measures it. The model only puts it into words — it never calculates a number.

📋

Show your working

Tap Sources under any answer to see exactly what was measured and which period it covers. An assistant that will not say what it looked at is one you are right not to trust.

🔒

Read-only

Ask can explain and compare, but it cannot add, edit, or delete anything in your plans. Nothing you ask can change your data.

🗑️

Not saved

The conversation is kept in memory only and is discarded when you close it. Nothing is stored, and nothing is synced.

🎯

One plan at a time

A pill at the top says which plan the answers are about, and switches between them. Suggested questions change with the last answer, so you can keep going without typing.

Ask needs Advanced Tools (or Lifetime Access) and a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings. It describes what your numbers show — it does not give financial advice.

🏠 Loans & Deposits

A budget is not the whole picture. Add a mortgage, a car loan, or a fixed deposit beside your plans, enter the terms from your statement, and see exactly where each one is heading — still with no bank login and nothing leaving your device.

🏠

Mortgages

Balance, rate, term, and monthly fees give you the estimated payment, the interest still to come, the next payment date, and an estimated payoff date. Charts cover the balance burn down, principal against interest, and how the rate moved.

🚗

Car Loans

Record the payments you actually make and the loan tells you whether you are ahead of, on, or behind its estimated schedule — not just what the lender's original schedule said.

💵

Fixed Deposits

Projected maturity value and interest, with simple, monthly, quarterly, annual, or at-maturity calculation so the projection matches your bank's actual product terms.

📋

The Full Schedule

Every scheduled payment, with its principal, interest, closing balance, and the rate in force that month. Rate changes are marked where they take effect.

📈

When the Rate Changes

Repricing is normal. Give the effective date and the new rate, and the schedule recalculates from the next scheduled payment onward — the payments you already made stay as they were.

📄

A Report You Can Send

Written analysis of cost, progress, and rate exposure, exported to PDF with the complete schedule and every tracked payment.

Kept separate on purpose

Loans and deposits take no day-to-day entries and never fold into budget totals, recaps, or plan reports. Your grocery spending and your mortgage stay different questions.

Browse by type

Once you keep more than one kind, the plan list groups by type and a filter dock switches between budgets, mortgages, car loans, and deposits. Tap a group's header to fold that type away — the list remembers. Keep only budgets and nothing changes.

Estimates, stated as estimates

Every figure is calculated from what you entered. Lender and bank statements vary with fees, rounding, payment timing, tax, and product rules, and the app labels these figures as estimates rather than presenting them as your bank's.

❤️ Budget & Goal Health

Automated health assessments in weekly or monthly Smart Digests that help you identify risks early and stay on track with your financial plans.

Requires: Advanced Tools
View Budget & Goal Health details

What Gets Assessed:

  • Budget Health - Spending against your plan's budget limit with category risk analysis
  • Goal Health - Pace tracking toward target dates and amounts for all active goals
  • Category Risks - Top 3 at-risk spending categories highlighted
  • Actionable Recommendations - Clear guidance based on current trends

Budget Health Status Levels:

✅
Good (Green)
Less than 80% of budget used
AND no categories over their limits

You're on track with healthy spending patterns. Continue monitoring to maintain this status.

⚠️
Watch (Orange)
Between 80-100% of budget used
OR 1-2 categories over their limits

Monitor spending closely to avoid exceeding your budget. Consider reducing discretionary spending.

🚨
At Risk (Red)
More than 100% of budget used
OR 3 or more categories over their limits

Immediate action needed to reduce spending. Review category risks and cut non-essential expenses.

Category Risk Analysis:

Budget health summaries automatically identify your top 3 at-risk spending categories based on:

  • Budget Limit Breaches - Categories that have exceeded their individual limits set in Goals
  • High Spend Percentage - Categories consuming a large percentage of total expenses
  • Trend Analysis - Categories showing rapid spending increases compared to historical patterns

This helps you focus on the specific areas where you can make the biggest impact on your overall budget health.

Goal Health Tracking:

✅
Good (Green)

On pace to meet target date OR already 100% complete

Your current savings rate will get you to your goal on time. Keep it up!
🚨
At Risk (Red)

Behind required pace OR overdue past target date

You need to increase your savings rate to meet your goal. See recommended daily/weekly/month amounts.
Goal Health Cards Show:
  • Required Savings Rate - What you need to save per day/week/month to hit the target
  • Actual Savings Rate - Your current pace based on historical data
  • Days Remaining - Time left until your target date
  • Percentage Complete - How far you've progressed toward the goal amount
  • Actionable Recommendations - Specific adjustments to get back on track
